## The process, in order

Use this sequence whenever a piece needs to tell the story. It is the spine of the
website's "field to bottle" section and works equally well in print.

1. **Seed** — varieties selected by hand: red, yellow, cherry, plus a black
   cultivar high in anthocyanin and lycopene.
2. **Nursery** — germinated in the farm's own nursery house with humidity and
   light controlled from day one; only strong seedlings move on.
3. **Growing** — insect netting and biological controls (sticky traps) instead of
   pesticides, so fruit is clean on the vine rather than washed before sale.
4. **Cold pressing** — temperature-controlled extraction to preserve lycopene and
   vitamins; the line runs at night for cheaper power.
5. **Bottling and QC** — packed on the same land it grew on, traceable back to the
   harvest it came from.

Soap follows its own path: handmade one bar at a time, then cured so a bar stays
sound for two years. It is the slowest process on the farm.

## Known gaps

The farm's own strategy document names these. They constrain what can honestly be
promised in any deliverable:

- Legal status, per-SKU FDA numbers, full INCI ingredient lists, production
  capacity, wholesale pricing and trademark registration are all unconfirmed.
- Sales run almost entirely through Facebook and LINE. No cart, no checkout.
- Content has recently leaned on AI generation and engagement has dropped.
- Production is family-scale and becomes the bottleneck if demand spikes.
